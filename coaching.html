Coaching Background

Coaching has been a central part of my professional development and has influenced the way I approach leadership, education, communication, and program development.

My experience includes more than a decade of coaching across tennis, basketball, and cross country, with roles at the collegiate, high school, junior high, youth, community recreation, and club levels.

I have worked with athletes at different stages of development, from beginners learning fundamental movement and sport skills to competitive athletes preparing for interscholastic, collegiate, USTA, and tournament environments.

Across these settings, my focus has remained consistent: create organized, challenging, supportive environments where athletes can improve, compete, and develop confidence and independence.

Coaching Philosophy

My approach to coaching combines teaching, athlete ownership, purposeful competition, and long-term development.

Teach the Athlete

Coaching should develop understanding, not dependency. Athletes should learn how to make decisions, solve problems, and take increasing ownership of their performance.

Train With Purpose

Practices should connect directly to competition. Activities should have clear objectives and create opportunities for athletes to apply skills under realistic conditions.

Build the Environment

Athlete development depends on culture. Expectations, communication, challenge, encouragement, and accountability all shape the quality of the sport experience.

Athletic Background

My perspective as a coach is also informed by my own experience as an athlete. I competed in multiple sports growing up and continued into collegiate tennis at Diablo Valley College.

Experiencing sport from both sides of the athlete-coach relationship has shaped the way I think about development, confidence, opportunity, competition, and the long-term impact that coaches can have.

I continue to participate in tennis and use my own experiences as a player to better understand the technical, tactical, physical, and psychological demands athletes encounter.
