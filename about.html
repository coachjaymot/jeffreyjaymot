About

A multidisciplinary career built around people, programs, and physical activity.

My professional path has crossed kinesiology, education, athletics, research, technology, business, and community programming. The common thread has always been developing people and building environments where they can succeed.

Professional Background

I am a kinesiology professional, educator, researcher, coach, and program leader with experience spanning secondary education, interscholastic athletics, collegiate athletics, community recreation, youth development, and sport programming.

My career began through teaching and coaching, where I developed a strong interest in the relationship between physical activity, education, leadership, and personal development. Over time, that work expanded into athletic administration, program management, curriculum development, research, technology, and organizational leadership.

I have worked in roles that required both direct service and systems-level thinking: teaching students, coaching athletes, managing athletic programs, coordinating events, developing community programs, supervising operations, and conducting research.

Rather than viewing these areas as separate careers, I see them as interconnected. Sport, education, recreation, research, and technology all provide opportunities to improve how people learn, move, participate, and engage with their communities.
