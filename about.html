About

A multidisciplinary career built around people, programs, and physical activity.

My professional path has crossed kinesiology, education, athletics, research, technology, business, and community programming. The common thread has always been developing people and building environments where they can succeed.

Professional Background

I am a kinesiology professional, educator, researcher, coach, and program leader with experience spanning secondary education, interscholastic athletics, collegiate athletics, community recreation, youth development, and sport programming.

My career began through teaching and coaching, where I developed a strong interest in the relationship between physical activity, education, leadership, and personal development. Over time, that work expanded into athletic administration, program management, curriculum development, research, technology, and organizational leadership.

I have worked in roles that required both direct service and systems-level thinking: teaching students, coaching athletes, managing athletic programs, coordinating events, developing community programs, supervising operations, and conducting research.

Rather than viewing these areas as separate careers, I see them as interconnected. Sport, education, recreation, research, and technology all provide opportunities to improve how people learn, move, participate, and engage with their communities.

The Career Thread

Although my experience spans several fields, four themes consistently connect my work.

Leadership

Building programs, coordinating people and resources, solving operational challenges, and creating systems that support long-term success.

Education

Helping people learn through teaching, coaching, curriculum development, mentorship, and applied instructional practice.

Physical Activity

Using sport, exercise, recreation, and movement as tools for development, health, participation, and community engagement.

Research & Analysis

Applying research, technology, and data to better understand programs, environments, participation, and decision-making.

Professional Approach

I approach leadership and program development through a combination of practical experience, evidence, adaptability, and relationship building.

01

People First

Strong programs begin with understanding the people they serve. I value communication, mentorship, accessibility, and meaningful relationships.

02

Systems Thinking

Sustainable improvement requires looking beyond individual tasks and understanding how people, policies, environments, resources, and operations interact.

03

Continuous Improvement

I believe strong programs evolve through reflection, data, feedback, experimentation, and a willingness to adjust when better approaches become available.

Current Direction

My current work continues to bring together kinesiology, research, sport, recreation, education, and program leadership. I am particularly interested in how organizations and communities can create stronger, more accessible opportunities for people to participate in physical activity.

Through doctoral research, community programming, coaching, and continued professional development, I am expanding my ability to work across both direct-service and organizational leadership environments.

Long term, my goal is to contribute to organizations where program development, leadership, physical activity, education, and community impact intersect.
